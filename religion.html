Overview

The largest religious groups by sect (by number of followers) include:

  • Sunni, ~1.8 billion
  • Catholicism, ~1.4 billion
  • Hinduism, ~1.2 billion
  • Protestantism, ~800 million
  • Mahayana, ~400 million
  • Orthodox, ~300 million
  • Shia, ~200 million
  • Shintō, ~100 million

In larger granularity, each of these consist of multiple subgroups, branches and/or denominations:

  • Sunni Islam (~1.8 billion)
    • Hanafi (~40-50%)
    • Shafi'i (~20-25%)
    • Maliki (~15-20%)
    • Hanbali (~5-10%)
  • Catholicism (~1.4 billion)
    • Latin Church (Roman Rite) (~98%)
    • Eastern Catholic Churches (~2%)
  • Hinduism (~1.2 billion)
    • Vaishnavism (~35-40%)
    • Shaivism (~25-30%)
    • Shaktism (~10-15%)
    • Smartism (~5-10%)
    • others
  • Protestantism (~800 million)
    • Lutheranism (~10-12%)
    • Anglicanism (~10-12%)
    • Reformed (Calvinism) (~10-12%)
    • Baptists (~12-15%)
    • Methodism (~5-8%)
    • Pentecostalism (~25-30%)
    • others
  • Mahayana (~300-350 million)
    • Zen (~10-15%)
    • Pure Land (~25-35%)
    • Nichiren (~5-10%)
  • Eastern Orthodox Christianity (~300 million)
    • Greek Orthodoxy (~10-15%)
    • Russian Orthodoxy (~60-70%)
    • Serbian Orthodoxy (~5-10%)
    • others
  • Shia Islam (~200 million)
    • Twelver Shia (~85-90%)
    • Ismailism (~5-10%)
    • Zaidism (~2-5%)
    • others
  • Theravada (~150-200 million)
    • Mahavihara tradition (~80-90%)
    • Forest tradition (~5-15%)
  • Shintō (~100 million)
    • State Shintō (historical influence)
    • Shintō sect groups
    • others
  • Vajrayana (~20-30 million)
    • Tibetan Buddhism (~70-85%)
    • Shingon Buddhism (~10-20%)
    • Newar Buddhism (~2-5%)
