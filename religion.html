The largest religious groups by sect (by number of followers) include:

  • Sunni, ~1.8 billion
  • Catholicism, ~1.4 billion
  • Hinduism, ~1.2 billion
  • Protestantism, ~800 million
  • Mahayana, ~400 million
  • Orthodoxy, ~300 million
  • Theravada, ~250 million
  • Shia, ~200 million
  • Shintō, ~100 million
